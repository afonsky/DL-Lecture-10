# Computer Vision in 2026: Three Useful Starting Points

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-5 text-sm">
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-900">

### Specialists for your label set

<br>

#### **YOLO26**  
##### CNN-based, native NMS-free inference; five sizes and several vision tasks.
<br>

#### **RF-DETR**  
##### DINOv2 + detection transformer; detection, instance masks, keypoints.

<div class="mt-3 font-semibold">Choose when target classes and deployment constraints are known.</div>
</div>
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-900">

### Reusable visual features

<br>

#### **DINOv3**  
##### Self-supervised backbones with strong dense features; ViT and distilled ConvNeXt variants.
<br>

#### **SigLIP 2**  
##### Multilingual image–text embeddings for zero-shot classification and retrieval.

<div class="mt-3 font-semibold">Choose when labels are scarce or class names live in text.</div>
</div>
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900">

### Promptable perception

<br>

#### **SAM 3.1**  
##### Text or visual prompts → boxes, instance masks, scores and video tracks for all matching concepts.

<div class="mt-3 font-semibold">Choose for interactive annotation, open-vocabulary instances and tracking.</div>
</div>
</div>

# Foundation Backbones: Start from Features, Not from Zero

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-7 text-sm">
<div>
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-900">

## DINOv3 · images only

- **Self-supervised:** LVD-1689M, **1.689 B** unlabeled images
- **ViT-7B/16:** **6,716 M** parameters
- **Distilled ViT:** 21 M / 29 M / 86 M / 300 M / 840 M
- **Distilled ConvNeXt:** 29 M / 50 M / 89 M / 198 M
- High-resolution **dense features** for classification, segmentation, depth and matching
</div>
<div class="mt-3 rounded p-3 bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-800">

#### **Practical start:** frozen features + linear probe; then compare with fine-tuning.
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-900">

## SigLIP 2 · images + text

- **Multilingual** vision–language encoders
- **ViT sizes:** B 86 M · L 303 M · So400m 400 M · g 1 B
- Zero-shot classification and image–text retrieval
- Better localization / dense features than SigLIP
- **NaFlex:** multiple resolutions and native aspect ratio
</div>
<div class="mt-3 rounded p-3 bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-800">

#### **Practical start:** write class names as text prompts; measure a zero-shot baseline before training a head.
</div>
</div>
</div>

<br>

<div class="mt-4 text-center">

**DINOv3:** “What are useful visual features?” · **SigLIP 2:** “How do images and language align?”
</div>

<br>

# SAM 3.1: Prompt a Concept, Get Every Matching Instance

<div class="grid grid-cols-[3fr_2fr] gap-7">
<div>
<div class="rounded-lg p-4 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900 text-center text-lg">

**“player in red” · box · point · mask exemplar**  
↓  
**SAM 3 detector + tracker, shared vision encoder**  
↓  
**all matching boxes + masks + scores + track IDs**
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 mt-4 text-sm">
<div class="rounded p-3 bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-800">

### **Open-vocabulary**  
#### Short text phrases or visual prompts
</div>
<div class="rounded p-3 bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-800">

### **Image + video**  
#### Segment, track and refine interactively
</div>
</div>

<div class="mt-4 rounded p-3 bg-orange-50 dark:bg-orange-900 text-sm">

#### **Not a semantic map of every class:** it finds instances matching the prompt, including negative prompts with no matches.
</div>

<span class="refs">Official sources: [SAM 3 model, outputs and benchmarks](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3) · [SAM 3.1 release notes](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3/blob/main/RELEASE_SAM3p1.md) · [SAM 3 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16719)</span>

</div>
<div class="text-sm">

### Specifications

- #### **848 M parameters** in SAM 3's detector + tracker
- #### SA-Co benchmark: **270 K unique concepts**
- #### Data engine: **4 M+ annotated concepts**
- #### SAM 3 reaches **75–80% of human performance** on SA-Co

<br>

### SAM 3.1 · March 2026

**Object Multiplex:** joint multi-object tracking in shared-memory buckets.

About **7× faster at 128 objects** on one H100 than the November 2025 SAM 3 release; accuracy is benchmark-dependent, not universally unchanged.
</div>
</div>
